Link the Sonorium app

This code links the Sonorium app on your phone to your Sonorium server.

With the app installed, scanning the code opens the app, and the app connects to your server on your home network.

The Sonorium app isn't on Google Play yet. The link to get it will be on this page when it is.

The details in the code, such as your server's address and the one-time pairing code, stay on your phone. They aren't sent to this website.
